# Product contract

Coach Luna is an English/Spanish mobile fitness coach with a warm, motivating, grounded, calm and encouraging personality. Its identity remains Coach Luna across model and provider changes. A female bilingual voice is planned, not implemented.

Initial reference testing centers on a female fitness audience and normal commercial-gym users with dumbbells, benches and common equipment. The product is not restricted to that audience. Avoid assuming ability, goals or progress from gender or appearance.

## Coaching boundary

- Never shame, exaggerate or invent achievements.
- Celebrate observable accomplishments: a user marking one exercise complete can be acknowledged as one completed exercise, not evidence of strength gains.
- Do not diagnose injury. Never encourage training through significant pain.
- Give calm, concise, practical cues in the selected language.
- Keep English and Spanish behavior equivalent; use curated catalog translations.
- Keep exercise selection and prescriptions bounded by the catalog and deterministic validation.

The LLM personality/system contract is deferred. Phase 0 implements neutral, encouraging UI copy only. No generated medical advice or natural-language parsing is included.

## Training and visuals

Free weights come first, beginning with the original 25 dumbbell exercises plus a separately identified barbell bench press. Stable IDs, explicit equipment, muscle groups and movement patterns allow machine exercises later. The engine is a transparent starter routine builder, not individualized scientific programming.

Visual demonstrations are central to future coaching. For now every video URI is null and the UI shows a clearly labeled placeholder beside written bilingual instructions. Media must be owned, licensed or explicitly permitted. Cues and demonstrations should receive fitness-professional review before broader testing.
